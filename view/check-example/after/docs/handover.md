# Handover

What the next person needs to know to go on.

## Where things stand

- The member page shows loans, history, reading lists and holds.
- Holds work: a member sees the place in the line, can pause a hold and can cancel it.
- The night run offers the copies that came back, and takes about five minutes for the whole library.
